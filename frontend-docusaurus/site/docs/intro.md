# Welcome to Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

Welcome to the comprehensive textbook on **Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics**. This course will take you from foundational concepts to building complete AI-powered robotic systems.

## What You'll Learn

This textbook covers the essential skills needed to develop modern humanoid robots and embodied AI systems:

### Module 1: ROS 2 Fundamentals
Master the Robot Operating System 2 (ROS 2), the industry-standard middleware for robotics development. You'll learn about nodes, topics, services, and actions.

### Module 2: Robot Simulation
Create and test robots in virtual environments using Gazebo and Unity Robotics. Learn to simulate sensors, actuators, and physical interactions.

### Module 3: NVIDIA Isaac Platform
Explore NVIDIA's powerful Isaac SDK and Isaac Sim for AI-accelerated robotics. Master sim-to-real transfer techniques to deploy trained models to physical robots.

### Module 4: Humanoid Robotics & AI
Build complete humanoid robot systems with Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, conversational interfaces, and integrate everything in a capstone project.

## Prerequisites

To get the most out of this textbook, you should have:

- **Python Programming**: Intermediate level (functions, classes, async)
- **Linear Algebra**: Vectors, matrices, transformations
- **Basic AI/ML**: Understanding of neural networks (helpful but not required)
- **Linux/Ubuntu**: Familiarity with command line

## Hardware Recommendations

For hands-on exercises:

- NVIDIA RTX GPU (3060 or better) for simulation and AI training
- Ubuntu 22.04 LTS (native or WSL2)
- 16GB+ RAM
- 50GB+ free disk space
